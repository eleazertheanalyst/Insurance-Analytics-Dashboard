## Project Overview

This project analyzes insurance data to evaluate policy performance, premium contributions, claim distribution, and customer demographics. The objective was to monitor key insurance KPIs, understand claim settlement patterns, and assess the overall performance of the insurance portfolio.

Microsoft Power BI was used to develop an interactive dashboard featuring financial KPIs, policy analysis, claim status breakdowns, premium distribution, and customer segmentation.

The final solution provides an overview of insurance operations and enables users to explore policy and claims performance through interactive visualizations and filters.

# Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this analysis were to:
- Monitor total premium, coverage, and claim amounts.
- Evaluate claim settlement performance.
- Analyze premium contributions across different policy types.
- Compare pending, rejected, and settled claims.
- Examine customer distribution by gender.
- Analyze claim amounts across different age groups.
- Monitor active and inactive insurance policies.
- Identify patterns in insurance policy and claims performance.

# Key Insights & Analytical Findings

### Overall Insurance Performance
- Total premium amount reached 5.97M.
- Total coverage amount stood at 600.33M.
- Total claim amount was 16.90M.
- Claim settlement status displayed approximately 202.95M.

**Insight:** The dashboard provides visibility into premium contributions, coverage exposure, and claim settlement performance, supporting the monitoring of overall insurance operations.

### Premium Analysis by Policy Type
- Travel insurance recorded the highest premium contribution at approximately 2.5M.
- Health insurance followed with approximately 1.2M.
- Auto insurance contributed approximately 1.0M.
- Life insurance accounted for approximately 0.7M.
- Home insurance recorded approximately 0.6M.

**Insight:** Travel insurance represents the largest premium-contributing policy category, followed by Health and Auto insurance.

### Claim Status Analysis
- Rejected claims accounted for approximately 4.4K.
- Settled claims accounted for approximately 3.4K.
- Pending claims accounted for approximately 2.3K.

**Insight:** Rejected claims represent the largest category by claim count, highlighting an area that may require further investigation into claim rejection patterns.

### Customer Demographics
- Female customers accounted for 5,000 (50%).
- Male customers accounted for 5,000 (50%).

**Insight:** The customer base is evenly distributed between male and female policyholders.

### Claim Amount by Age Group
- Adults recorded approximately 8.8M in claims.
- Elder customers accounted for approximately 6.4M.
- Young Adults recorded approximately 1.7M.

**Insight:** Adult customers represent the largest share of claim amounts, followed by Elder customers and Young Adults.

### Active & Inactive Policies
- Active policies: 5.82K (58.21%).
- Inactive policies: 4.18K (41.79%).

**Insight:** Approximately 58.21% of policies are active, while 41.79% are inactive, providing an opportunity to further examine policy retention and customer engagement.
